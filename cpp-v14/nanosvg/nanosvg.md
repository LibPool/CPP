# nanosvg

> 标签: Zlib, c++, conan, nanosvg, vcpkg

## 简介

NanoSVG is a simple stupid single-header-file SVG parser. It is accompanied by a simple SVG rasterizer.

Conan Center 收录：NanoSVG is a simple stupid single-header-file SVG parser.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
