# libmysofa

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, libmysofa, vcpkg

## 简介

Reader for AES SOFA files to get better HRTFs (Head-Relative Transfer Functions)

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
