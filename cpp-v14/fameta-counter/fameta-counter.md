# fameta-counter

> 标签: Unlicense, c++, fameta-counter, vcpkg

## 简介

Compile time counter that works with all major modern compilers, compatible with C++11 and above.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
