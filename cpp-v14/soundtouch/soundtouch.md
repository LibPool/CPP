# soundtouch

> 标签: LGPL-2.1-or-later, c++, conan, soundtouch, vcpkg

## 简介

SoundTouch is an open-source audio processing library for changing the Tempo, Pitch and Playback Rates of audio streams or audio files.

Conan Center 收录：an open-source audio processing library that allows changing the sound tempo, pitch and playback rate parameters independently

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
