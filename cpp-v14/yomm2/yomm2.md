# yomm2

> 标签: BSL-1.0, c++, conan, vcpkg, yomm2

## 简介

YOMM2 is an implementation of open multi-methods.

Conan Center 收录：Fast, orthogonal, open multi-methods. Solve the Expression Problem in C++17

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
