# ecos

> 标签: GPL-3.0-or-later, c++, conan, ecos, vcpkg

## 简介

A lightweight conic solver for second-order cone programming.

Conan Center 收录：ECOS is a numerical software for solving convex second-order cone programs (SOCPs).

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
