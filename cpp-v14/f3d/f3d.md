# f3d

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, f3d, vcpkg

## 简介

A fast and minimalist 3D viewer

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

- vcpkg 端口：https://vcpkg.io/en/packages/f3d
- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/f3d
