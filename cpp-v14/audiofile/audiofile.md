# audiofile

> 标签: MIT, audiofile, c++, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

A simple header-only C++ library for reading and writing audio files.

Conan Center 收录：A simple C++11 library for reading and writing audio files.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
