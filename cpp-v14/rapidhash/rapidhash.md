# rapidhash

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause, c++, rapidhash, vcpkg

## 简介

Very fast, high quality, platform independent hashing algorithm.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
