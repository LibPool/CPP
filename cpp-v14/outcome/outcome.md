# outcome

> 标签: Apache-2.0 OR BSL-1.0, c++, outcome, vcpkg

## 简介

Provides very lightweight outcome<T> and result<T> (non-Boost edition)

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
