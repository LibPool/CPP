# libunwind

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, libunwind, vcpkg

## 简介

Unix libray for portable stack unwinding

Conan Center 收录：Manipulate the preserved state of each call-frame and resume the execution at any point.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
