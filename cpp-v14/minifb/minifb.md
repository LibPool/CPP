# minifb

> 标签: MIT, c++, minifb, vcpkg

## 简介

MiniFB (Mini FrameBuffer) is a small cross platform library that makes it easy to render (32-bit) pixels in a window.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
