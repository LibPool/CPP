# pugixml

> 标签: MIT, c++, pugixml, vcpkg

## 简介

Light-weight, simple and fast XML parser for C++ with XPath support

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
