# libcap

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause OR GPL-2.0-only, c++, libcap, vcpkg

## 简介

A library for getting and setting POSIX.1e (formerly POSIX 6) draft 15 capabilities.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
