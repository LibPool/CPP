# opencascade

> 标签: LGPL-2.1-only, LGPL-2.1-or-later, c++, conan, opencascade, vcpkg

## 简介

Open CASCADE Technology (OCCT) is an open-source software development platform for 3D CAD, CAM, CAE.

Conan Center 收录：A software development platform providing services for 3D

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
