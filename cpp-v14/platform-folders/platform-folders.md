# platform-folders

> 标签: MIT, c++, platform-folders, vcpkg

## 简介

A C++ library to look for special directories like "My Documents" and "%APPDATA%"

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网
