# pngpp

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, pngpp, vcpkg

## 简介

A C++ wrapper for libpng library.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- vcpkg 端口：https://vcpkg.io/en/packages/pngpp
- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/pngpp
- 官网（Conan）：https://www.nongnu.org/pngpp
- Conan Center：https://conan.io/center/recipes/pngpp
- Conan recipe 源码：https://github.com/conan-io/conan-center-index/tree/master/recipes/pngpp
