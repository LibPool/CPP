# mexce

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause, c++, conan, mexce, vcpkg

## 简介

Header-only JIT compiler for scalar mathematical expressions.

Conan Center 收录：Single-header, dependency-free JIT compiler for scalar mathematical expressions.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
