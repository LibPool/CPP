# tlx

> 标签: c++, tlx, vcpkg

## 简介

tlx is a collection of C++ helpers and extensions universally needed, but not found in the STL

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
