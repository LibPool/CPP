# robin-hood-hashing

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, robin-hood-hashing, vcpkg

## 简介

Fast & memory efficient hashtable based on robin hood hashing for C++11/14/17/20

Conan Center 收录：Faster and more efficient replacement for std::unordered_map / std::unordered_set

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
