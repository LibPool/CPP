# stella-cv-fbow

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, stella-cv-fbow, vcpkg

## 简介

This is a modified version of the original Fast Bag of Words by @rmsalinas.

Conan Center 收录：FBoW (Fast Bag of Words) is an extremely optimized version of the DBoW2/DBoW3 libraries.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
