# ez8-co-yapi

> 标签: MIT, c++, ez8-co-yapi, vcpkg

## 简介

A header-only fusion injector that reduces differences between x64, wow64 and x86 processes.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
