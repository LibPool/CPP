# raylib

> 标签: Zlib, c++, conan, raylib, vcpkg

## 简介

A simple and easy-to-use library to enjoy videogames programming

Conan Center 收录：raylib is a simple and easy-to-use library to enjoy videogames programming.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

vcpkg port 目录：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/raylib
- vcpkg 许可证：Zlib
- 平台/支持条件：!arm32 & !uwp
