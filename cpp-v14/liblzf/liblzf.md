# liblzf

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause, BSD-2-Clause OR GPL-2.0-or-later, c++, conan, liblzf, vcpkg

## 简介

LZF is an extremely fast compression algorithm. It is ideal for applications where you want to save some space but not at the cost of speed.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
