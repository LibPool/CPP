# kalshi-cpp

> 标签: MIT, c++, kalshi-cpp, vcpkg

## 简介

C++23 client for Kalshi's Predictions API: every REST operation and WebSocket channel.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
