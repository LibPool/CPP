# vvenc

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, BSD-3-Clause-Clear, c++, conan, vcpkg, vvenc

## 简介

VVenC is a fast and efficient H.266/VVC encoder implementation.

Conan Center 收录：Fraunhofer Versatile Video Encoder (VVenC)

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
