# catch2

> 标签: BSL-1.0, c++, catch2, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

A modern, C++-native, test framework for unit-tests, TDD and BDD.

Conan Center 收录：A modern, C++-native, header-only, framework for unit-tests, TDD and BDD

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
