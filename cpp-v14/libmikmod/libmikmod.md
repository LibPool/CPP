# libmikmod

> 标签: LGPL-2.1-or-later, c++, libmikmod, vcpkg

## 简介

Mikmod is a module player and library supporting many formats, including mod, s3m, it, and xm.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
