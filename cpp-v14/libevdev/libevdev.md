# libevdev

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, libevdev, vcpkg

## 简介

Wrapper library for evdev devices

Conan Center 收录：libevdev is a wrapper library for evdev devices.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
