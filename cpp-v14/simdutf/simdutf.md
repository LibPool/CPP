# simdutf

> 标签: Apache-2.0 OR MIT, c++, conan, simdutf, vcpkg

## 简介

Unicode validation and transcoding at billions of characters per second

Conan Center 收录：Unicode routines (UTF8, UTF16, UTF32) and base64 at billions of characters per second.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
