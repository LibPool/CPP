# dylib

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, dylib, vcpkg

## 简介

C++ cross-platform wrapper around dynamic loading of shared libraries

Conan Center 收录：C++ cross-platform wrapper around dynamic loading of shared libraries (dll, so, dylib)

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
