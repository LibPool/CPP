# observer-ptr-lite

> 标签: c++, observer-ptr-lite, vcpkg

## 简介

A C++17-like observer_ptr for C++98 and later in a single-file header-only library

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
