# openmvg

> 标签: c++, openmvg, vcpkg

## 简介

open Multiple View Geometry library. Basis for 3D computer vision and Structure from Motion.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
