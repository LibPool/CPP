# dav1d

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause, BSD-2-Clause AND ISC, c++, conan, dav1d, vcpkg

## 简介

dav1d is a new open-source AV1 decoder developed by the VideoLAN and FFmpeg communities and sponsored by the Alliance for Open Media.

Conan Center 收录：dav1d is a new AV1 cross-platform decoder, open-source, and focused on speed, size and correctness.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
