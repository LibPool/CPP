# blickfeld-qb2

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, blickfeld-qb2, c++, vcpkg

## 简介

Client library to communicate securely with Qb2 LiDAR devices of Blickfeld GmbH.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
