# libidn2

> 标签: GPL-3.0-or-later, c++, conan, libidn2, vcpkg

## 简介

GNU Libidn is an implementation of the Stringprep, Punycode and IDNA 2003 specifications. Libidn's purpose is to encode and decode internationalized domain names.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
