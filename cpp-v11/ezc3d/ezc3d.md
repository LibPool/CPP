# ezc3d

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, ezc3d, vcpkg

## 简介

C3D reader/writer

Conan Center 收录：EZC3D is an easy to use reader, modifier and writer for C3D format files.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
