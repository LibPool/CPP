# luau

> 标签: MIT, c++, luau, vcpkg

## 简介

A fast, small, safe, gradually typed embeddable scripting language derived from Lua

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
