# nonstd-bit-lite

> 标签: BSL-1.0, c++, nonstd-bit-lite, vcpkg

## 简介

C++20/C++23 bit operations for C++98 and later in a single-file header-only library

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网
