# arpack-ng

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, arpack-ng, c++, vcpkg

## 简介

ARPACK-NG is a collection of Fortran77 subroutines designed to solve large scale eigenvalue problems.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
