# dbus

> 标签: (AFL-2.1 OR GPL-2.0-or-later) AND DocumentRef-COPYING, AFL-2.1 OR GPL-2.0-or-later, c++, conan, dbus, vcpkg

## 简介

D-Bus specification and reference implementation, including libdbus and dbus-daemon

Conan Center 收录：D-Bus is a simple system for interprocess communication and coordination.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
