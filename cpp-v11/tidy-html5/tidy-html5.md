# tidy-html5

> 标签: HTMLTIDY, c++, conan, tidy-html5, vcpkg

## 简介

Tidy tidies HTML and XML. It can tidy your documents by itself, and developers can easily integrate its features into even more powerful tools.

Conan Center 收录：The granddaddy of HTML tools, with support for modern standards

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
