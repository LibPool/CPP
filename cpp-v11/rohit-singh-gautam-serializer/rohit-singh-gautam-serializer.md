# rohit-singh-gautam-serializer

> 标签: GPL-3.0-or-later AND 0BSD, c++, rohit-singh-gautam-serializer, vcpkg

## 简介

C++20 schema compiler and serialization runtime with multi-language output, JSON, binary, Protobuf codecs, managed snapshots, collaboration, and compression.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
