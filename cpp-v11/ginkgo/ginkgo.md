# ginkgo

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, ginkgo, vcpkg

## 简介

Ginkgo is a high-performance linear algebra library for manycore systems, with a focus on sparse solution of linear systems.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
