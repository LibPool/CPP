# libraw

> 标签: (LGPL-2.1-only OR CDDL-1.0) AND BSD-3-Clause, CDDL-1.0, c++, conan, libraw, vcpkg

## 简介

raw image decoder library

Conan Center 收录：LibRaw is a library for reading RAW files obtained from digital photo cameras (CRW/CR2, NEF, RAF, DNG, and others).

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
