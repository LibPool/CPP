## 简介

SZ3: a modular error-bounded lossy compressor for scientific floating-point data. Header-only C++17 library, with an optional command-line tool and HDF5 filter.

Conan Center 收录：SZ3: a modular error-bounded lossy compressor for scientific floating-point data

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
