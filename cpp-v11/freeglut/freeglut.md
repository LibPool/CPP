# freeglut

> 标签: X11, c++, conan, freeglut, vcpkg

## 简介

A free OpenGL utility toolkit, the open-sourced alternative to the GLUT library.

Conan Center 收录：Open-source alternative to the OpenGL Utility Toolkit (GLUT) library

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
