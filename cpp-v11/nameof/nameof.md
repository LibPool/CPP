# nameof

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, nameof, vcpkg

## 简介

Nameof operator for modern C++, simply obtain the name of a variable, type, function, macro, and enum.

Conan Center 收录：Header-only C++17 library provides nameof macros and functions to simply obtain the name of a variable, type, function, macro, and enum.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
