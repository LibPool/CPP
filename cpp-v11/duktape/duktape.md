# duktape

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, duktape, vcpkg

## 简介

Embeddable Javascript engine with a focus on portability and compact footprint.

Conan Center 收录：Duktape is an embeddable Javascript engine, with a focus on portability and compact footprint.
