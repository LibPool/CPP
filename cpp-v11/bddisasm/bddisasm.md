# bddisasm

> 标签: Apache-2.0, bddisasm, c++, vcpkg

## 简介

bddisasm is a fast, lightweight, x86/x64 instruction decoder and emulator.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/bitdefender/bddisasm
- vcpkg 端口：https://vcpkg.io/en/packages/bddisasm
- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/bddisasm
