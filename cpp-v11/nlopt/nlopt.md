# nlopt

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, nlopt, vcpkg

## 简介

Library for nonlinear local and global optimization, for functions with and without gradient information.

Conan Center 收录：Library for nonlinear optimization, wrapping many

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
