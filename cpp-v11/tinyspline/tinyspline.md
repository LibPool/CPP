# tinyspline

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, tinyspline, vcpkg

## 简介

Library for NURBS, B-Splines, and Bézier curves, allowing you to handle splines with ease

Conan Center 收录：Library for interpolating, transforming, and querying

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
