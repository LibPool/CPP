# xpack

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, conan, vcpkg, xpack

## 简介

Convert C++ struct from/to json/xml

Conan Center 收录：C++ reflection, ability to convert between C++ structures and json/xml.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
