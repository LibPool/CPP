# openimageio

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, openimageio, vcpkg

## 简介

A library for reading and writing images, and a bunch of related classes, utilities, and application.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网
