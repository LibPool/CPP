# pagmo2

> 标签: GPL-3.0-or-later OR LGPL-3.0-or-later, c++, conan, pagmo2, vcpkg

## 简介

A C++ platform to perform parallel computations of optimization tasks (global and local) via the asynchronous generalized island model.

Conan Center 收录：pagmo is a C++ scientific library for massively parallel optimization.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
