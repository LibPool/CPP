# ktx

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, conan, ktx, vcpkg

## 简介

The Khronos KTX library and tools.
Functions for writing and reading KTX files, and instantiating OpenGL®, OpenGL ES™️ and Vulkan® textures from them.

Conan Center 收录：Khronos Texture library and tool.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
