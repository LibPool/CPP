# cctag

> 标签: MPL-2.0, c++, cctag, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

Computer vision library for detecting CCTag markers made up of concentric circles

Conan Center 收录：Detection of CCTag markers made up of concentric circles.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
