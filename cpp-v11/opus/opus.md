# opus

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, opus, vcpkg

## 简介

Totally open, royalty-free, highly versatile audio codec

Conan Center 收录：Opus is a totally open, royalty-free, highly versatile audio codec.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
