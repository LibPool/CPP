# neargye-semver

> 标签: MIT, c++, neargye-semver, vcpkg

## 简介

C++17 header-only dependency-free versioning library complying with Semantic Versioning 2.0.0

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网
