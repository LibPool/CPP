# cereal

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, cereal, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

a header-only C++11 serialization library (built in support for binary, XML and JSon)

Conan Center 收录：Serialization header-only library for C++11.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
