# idyntree

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, idyntree, vcpkg

## 简介

Multibody Dynamics Library designed for Free Floating Robots.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
