# clblast

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, clblast, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

A modern, lightweight, performant and tunable OpenCL BLAS library written in C++11.

Conan Center 收录：CLBlast is a lightweight, performant and tunable OpenCL BLAS library written in C++11.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
