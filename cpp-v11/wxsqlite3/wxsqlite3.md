# wxsqlite3

> 标签: LGPL-3.0+ WITH WxWindows-exception-3.1, c++, conan, wxsqlite3

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：wxSQLite3 is a C++ wrapper around the SQLite database designed for use in wxWidgets applications.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
