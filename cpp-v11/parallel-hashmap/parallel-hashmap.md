# parallel-hashmap

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, parallel-hashmap, vcpkg

## 简介

A header-only, very fast and memory-friendly family of C++ hash map & btree containers.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- vcpkg 端口：https://vcpkg.io/en/packages/parallel-hashmap
- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/parallel-hashmap
