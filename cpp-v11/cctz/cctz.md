# cctz

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, cctz, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

two libraries that cooperate with <chrono> to give C++ programmers all the necessary tools for computing with dates, times, and time zones in a simple and correct manner.

Conan Center 收录：C++ library for translating between absolute and civil times

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
