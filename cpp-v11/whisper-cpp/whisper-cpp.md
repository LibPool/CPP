# whisper-cpp

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, vcpkg, whisper-cpp

## 简介

Port of OpenAI's Whisper model in C/C++

Conan Center 收录：High-performance inference of OpenAI's Whisper automatic speech recognition (ASR) model

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
