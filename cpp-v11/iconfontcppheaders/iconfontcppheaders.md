# iconfontcppheaders

> 标签: Zlib, c++, conan, iconfontcppheaders

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：Headers for icon fonts Font Awesome, Fork Awesome, Google Material Design Icons, Google Material Design Symbols, Pictogrammers Material Design icons, Kenney game icons, Fontaudio, Codicons and Lucide.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
