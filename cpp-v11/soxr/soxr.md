# soxr

> 标签: LGPL-2.1-or-later, c++, conan, soxr, vcpkg

## 简介

High quality audio resampling

Conan Center 收录：The SoX Resampler library libsoxr performs fast, high-quality one-dimensional sample rate conversion.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
