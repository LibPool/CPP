# wavpack

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, vcpkg, wavpack

## 简介

WavPack encode/decode library, command-line programs, and several plugins

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
