# tinkerforge-bindings

> 标签: CC0 1.0 Universal, c++, conan, tinkerforge-bindings

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：API bindings to control Tinkerforge's Bricks and Bricklets

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- 官网（Conan）：https://www.tinkerforge.com/

- Conan recipe 源码：https://github.com/conan-io/conan-center-index/tree/master/recipes/tinkerforge-bindings
