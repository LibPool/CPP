# libdivide

> 标签: BSL-1.0 OR Zlib, c++, conan, libdivide, vcpkg

## 简介

libdivide.h is a header-only C/C++ library for optimizing integer division.

Conan Center 收录：Header-only C/C++ library for optimizing integer division.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
