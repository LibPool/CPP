# libfork

> 标签: MPL-2.0, c++, conan, libfork, vcpkg

## 简介

A bleeding-edge, lock-free, wait-free, continuation-stealing fork-join library built on C++20's coroutines.

Conan Center 收录：A bleeding-edge, lock-free, wait-free, continuation-stealing tasking library.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
