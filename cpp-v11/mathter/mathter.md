# mathter

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, mathter, vcpkg

## 简介

A flexible and fast matrix, transform, and geometry library.

Conan Center 收录：Powerful 3D math and small-matrix linear algebra library for games and science.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
