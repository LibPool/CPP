# imgui-sfml

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, imgui-sfml, vcpkg

## 简介

ImGui binding for use with SFML

Conan Center 收录：ImGui-SFML integrates Dear ImGui with SFML, enabling easy creation of GUIs for SFML applications.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
