# pexports

> 标签: GPL-2.0-or-later, c++, conan, pexports

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：pexports is a program to extract exported symbols from a PE image (executable).

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
