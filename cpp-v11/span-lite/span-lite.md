# span-lite

> 标签: BSL-1.0, c++, conan, span-lite, vcpkg

## 简介

A C++20-like span for C++98, C++11 and later in a single-file header-only library

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
