# gdbm

> 标签: GPL-3.0-or-later, c++, conan, gdbm, vcpkg

## 简介

GDBM is a library of database functions that use extensible hashing and works similar to the standard UNIX dbm.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
