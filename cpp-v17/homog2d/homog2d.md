# homog2d

> 标签: MPL-2.0, c++, conan, homog2d

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：C++ 2D geometry library, handles points, lines, polylines, planar transformations(and other primitives), using homogeneous coordinates.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
