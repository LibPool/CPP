# eudev

> 标签: GPL-2.0-or-later, c++, conan, eudev

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：eudev is a standalone dynamic and persistent device naming support (aka userspace devfs) daemon that runs independently from the init system.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
