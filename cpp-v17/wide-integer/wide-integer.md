# wide-integer

> 标签: BSL-1.0, c++, conan, wide-integer

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：Wide-Integer implements a generic C++ template for uint128_t, uint256_t, uint512_t, uint1024_t, etc.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
