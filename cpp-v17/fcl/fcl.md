# fcl

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, fcl, vcpkg

## 简介

A library for performing three types of proximity queries on a pair of geometric models composed of triangles

Conan Center 收录：C++11 library for performing three types of proximity

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
