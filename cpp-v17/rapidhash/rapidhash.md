# rapidhash

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause, c++, conan, rapidhash, vcpkg

## 简介

Very fast, high quality, platform independent hashing algorithm.

Conan Center 收录：Very fast, high quality, platform-independent hashing algorithm

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
