# brigand

> 标签: BSL-1.0, brigand, c++, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

Brigand is a light-weight, fully functional, instant-compile time C++ 11 meta-programming library.

Conan Center 收录：A light-weight, fully functional, instant-compile time meta-programming library.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
