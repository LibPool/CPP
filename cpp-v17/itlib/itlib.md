# itlib

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, itlib, vcpkg

## 简介

A collection of std-like single-header C++ libraries.

Conan Center 收录：A collection of small single-header C++ libraries similar to or extending the C++ standard library.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
