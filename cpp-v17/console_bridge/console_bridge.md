# console_bridge

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, console_bridge

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：A ROS-independent library for logging that seamlessly pipes into rosconsole/rosout for ROS-dependent packages

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

- Conan recipe 源码：https://github.com/conan-io/conan-center-index/tree/master/recipes/console_bridge
