# rapidyaml

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, rapidyaml

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：a library to parse and emit YAML, and do it fast.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

- 官网（Conan）：https://github.com/biojppm/rapidyaml
- Conan Center：https://conan.io/center/recipes/rapidyaml
- Conan recipe 源码：https://github.com/conan-io/conan-center-index/tree/master/recipes/rapidyaml
