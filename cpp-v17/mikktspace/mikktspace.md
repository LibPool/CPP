# mikktspace

> 标签: Zlib, c++, conan, mikktspace, vcpkg

## 简介

A common standard for tangent space used in baking tools to produce normal maps

Conan Center 收录：A common standard for tangent space used in baking tools to produce normal maps.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
