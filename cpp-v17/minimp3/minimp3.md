# minimp3

> 标签: CC0-1.0, c++, conan, minimp3, vcpkg

## 简介

Minimalistic, single-header library for decoding MP3. minimp3 is designed to be small, fast (with SSE and NEON support), and accurate (ISO conformant).

Conan Center 收录：Minimalistic MP3 decoder single header library.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
