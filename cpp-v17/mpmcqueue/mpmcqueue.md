# mpmcqueue

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, mpmcqueue, vcpkg

## 简介

A bounded multi-producer multi-consumer lock-free queue written in C++11

Conan Center 收录：A bounded multi-producer multi-consumer concurrent queue written in C++11.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
