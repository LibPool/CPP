# rappture

> 标签: c++, rappture, vcpkg

## 简介

Rappture is a toolkit supporting Rapid application infrastructure, making it quick and easy to develop powerful scientific applications.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
