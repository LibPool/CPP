# highs

> 标签: MIT, MIT AND BSD-3-Clause AND Zlib, c++, conan, highs, vcpkg

## 简介

High performance library to solve linear, mixed-integer, and convex quadratic optimization problems.

Conan Center 收录：high performance serial and parallel solver for large scale sparse linear optimization problems

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
