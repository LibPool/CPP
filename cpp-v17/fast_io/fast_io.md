# fast_io

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, fast_io

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：fast_io is a C++ io exception-safe RAII library based on C++ 20 Concepts. It is at least 10 times faster than cstdio than iostream.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
