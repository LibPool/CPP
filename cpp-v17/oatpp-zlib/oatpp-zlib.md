# oatpp-zlib

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, oatpp-zlib, vcpkg

## 简介

Oat++ functionality for automatically compressing/decompressing content with deflate and gzip.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
