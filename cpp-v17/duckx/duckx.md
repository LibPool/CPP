# duckx

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, duckx, vcpkg

## 简介

DuckX is a library for creation of Office docx files.

Conan Center 收录：C++ library for creating and updating Microsoft Word (.docx) files.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
