# swaggercpp

> 标签: MIT, c++, swaggercpp, vcpkg

## 简介

Professional C++23 library for Swagger/OpenAPI parsing, validation, traversal, and serialization.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
