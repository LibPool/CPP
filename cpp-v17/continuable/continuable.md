# continuable

> 标签: MIT, c++, continuable, vcpkg

## 简介

C++14 asynchronous allocation aware futures (supporting then, exception handling, coroutines and connections)

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
