# libbacktrace

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, libbacktrace, vcpkg

## 简介

The libbacktrace library may be linked into a program or library and used to produce symbolic backtraces.

Conan Center 收录：A C library that may be linked into a C/C++ program to produce symbolic backtraces.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
