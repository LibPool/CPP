# genann

> 标签: Zlib, c++, genann, vcpkg

## 简介

Genann is a minimal, well-tested library for training and using feedforward artificial neural networks (ANN) in C.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
