# pulseaudio

> 标签: LGPL-2.1, c++, conan, pulseaudio, vcpkg

## 简介

PulseAudio is a sound server, originally created to overcome the limitations of the Enlightened Sound Daemon (EsounD)

Conan Center 收录：PulseAudio is a sound system for POSIX OSes, meaning that it is a proxy for sound applications.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
