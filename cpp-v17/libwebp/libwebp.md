# libwebp

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, libwebp, vcpkg

## 简介

WebP codec: library to encode and decode images in WebP format

Conan Center 收录：Library to encode and decode images in WebP format

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
