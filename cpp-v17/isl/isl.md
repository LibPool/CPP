# isl

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, isl

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：isl is a library for manipulating sets and relations of integer points bounded by linear constraints.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
