# libmikmod

> 标签: LGPL-2.1-or-later, c++, conan, libmikmod, vcpkg

## 简介

Mikmod is a module player and library supporting many formats, including mod, s3m, it, and xm.

Conan Center 收录：Module player and library supporting many formats, including mod, s3m, it, and xm.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
