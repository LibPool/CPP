# libdisasm

> 标签: ClArtistic, MIT, c++, conan, libdisasm, vcpkg

## 简介

x86 Disassembler Library.

Conan Center 收录：The libdisasm library provides basic disassembly of Intel x86 instructions from a binary stream.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
