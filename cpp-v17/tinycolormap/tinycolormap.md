# tinycolormap

> 标签: MIT, MIT AND Apache-2.0, c++, conan, tinycolormap, vcpkg

## 简介

A header-only, single-file library for colormaps written in C++11.

Conan Center 收录：A header-only, single-file library for colormaps written in C++11

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
