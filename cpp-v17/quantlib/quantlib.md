# quantlib

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, quantlib, vcpkg

## 简介

The QuantLib C++ library

Conan Center 收录：QuantLib is a free/open-source library for modeling, trading, and risk management in real-life.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
