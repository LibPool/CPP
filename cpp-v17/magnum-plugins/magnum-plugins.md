# magnum-plugins

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, magnum-plugins, vcpkg

## 简介

Plugins for magnum, C++11/C++14 graphics middleware for games and data visualization

Conan Center 收录：Plugins for the Magnum C++11/C++14 graphics engine

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
