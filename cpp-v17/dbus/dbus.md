# dbus

> 标签: AFL-2.1 OR GPL-2.0-or-later, c++, dbus, vcpkg

## 简介

D-Bus specification and reference implementation, including libdbus and dbus-daemon

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
