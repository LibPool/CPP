# so5extra

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, so5extra, vcpkg

## 简介

A set of additional tools for SObjectizer framework.

Conan Center 收录：A collection of various SObjectizer's extensions.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
