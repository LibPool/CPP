# h5py-lzf

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, h5py-lzf, vcpkg

## 简介

The LZF filter is an alternative DEFLATE-style compressor for HDF5 datasets.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
