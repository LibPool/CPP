# ompl

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, ompl, vcpkg

## 简介

The Open Motion Planning Library, consists of many state-of-the-art sampling-based motion planning algorithms

Conan Center 收录：The Open Motion Planning Library (OMPL)

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
