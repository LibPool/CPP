# function2

> 标签: BSL-1.0, c++, conan, function2, vcpkg

## 简介

Improved drop-in replacement to std::function

Conan Center 收录：Improved and configurable drop-in replacement to std::function that supports move only types, multiple overloads and more

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
