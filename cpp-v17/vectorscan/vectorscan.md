# vectorscan

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, vcpkg, vectorscan

## 简介

A portable fork of the high-performance regular expression matching library

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
