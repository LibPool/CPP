# libarchive

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause, c++, conan, libarchive, vcpkg

## 简介

Library for reading and writing streaming archives

Conan Center 收录：Multi-format archive and compression library

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
