# autodock-vina

> 标签: autodock-vina, c++, vcpkg

## 简介

AutoDock Vina is one of the fastest and most widely used open-source docking engines.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
