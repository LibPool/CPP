# visit-struct

> 标签: BSL-1.0, c++, vcpkg, visit-struct

## 简介

A header-only library providing structure visitors for C++11 and C++14

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- vcpkg 端口：https://vcpkg.io/en/packages/visit-struct
- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/visit-struct
