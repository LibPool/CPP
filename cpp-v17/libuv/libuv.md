# libuv

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, MIT, c++, conan, libuv, vcpkg

## 简介

libuv is a multi-platform support library with a focus on asynchronous I/O.

Conan Center 收录：A multi-platform support library with a focus on asynchronous I/O

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
