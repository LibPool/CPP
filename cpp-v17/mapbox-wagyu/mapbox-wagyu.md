# mapbox-wagyu

> 标签: c++, mapbox-wagyu, vcpkg

## 简介

A general library for geometry operations of union, intersections, difference, and xor

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
