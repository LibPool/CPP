# mailio

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause, c++, conan, mailio, vcpkg

## 简介

mailio is a cross platform C++ library for MIME format and SMTP, POP3 and IMAP protocols. It is based on the standard C++ 17 and Boost library.

Conan Center 收录：mailio is a cross platform C++ library for MIME format and SMTP, POP3 and IMAP protocols.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
