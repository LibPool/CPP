# jwsung91-unilink

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, jwsung91-unilink, vcpkg

## 简介

Deprecated alias for wirestead. unilink was renamed to Wirestead; this port installs nothing itself and only depends on wirestead to avoid conflicting with its installed files.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
