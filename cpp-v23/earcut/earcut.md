# earcut

> 标签: ISC, c++, conan, earcut

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：A C++ port of earcut.js, a fast, header-only polygon triangulation library.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- 官网（Conan）：https://github.com/mapbox/earcut.hpp
- Conan Center：https://conan.io/center/recipes/earcut
- Conan recipe 源码：https://github.com/conan-io/conan-center-index/tree/master/recipes/earcut
