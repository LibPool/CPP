# geotrans

> 标签: NGA GEOTRANS ToS (https://earth-info.nga.mil/php/download.php?file=wgs-terms), c++, conan, geotrans, vcpkg

## 简介

GEOTRANS is an application that allows you to convert geographic coordinates among a wide variety of coordinate systems, map projections, grids, and datums. GEOTRANS runs in Microsoft Windows and LINUX environments.

Conan Center 收录：MSP GEOTRANS is the NGA and DOD approved coordinate converter and datum translator.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
