# graphthewy

> 标签: EUPL-1.2, c++, conan, graphthewy

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：Simple header-only C++ Library for graph modelling (directed or not) and graph cycle detection.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
