# dpp

> 标签: Apache-2.0 AND BSD-2-Clause AND BSD-3-Clause AND MIT, c++, dpp, vcpkg

## 简介

D++ Extremely Lightweight C++ Discord Library.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

- vcpkg 端口：https://vcpkg.io/en/packages/dpp
- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/dpp
