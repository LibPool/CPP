# opene57

> 标签: c++, conan, opene57

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：A C++ library for reading and writing E57 files, a fork of the original libE57 (http://libe57.org)

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
