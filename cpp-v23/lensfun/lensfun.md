# lensfun

> 标签: c++, lensfun, vcpkg

## 简介

Provide a open source database of photographic lenses and their characteristics

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
