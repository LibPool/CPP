# fdk-aac

> 标签: FDK-AAC, c++, fdk-aac, vcpkg

## 简介

A standalone Third-Party Modified Version of the Fraunhofer FDK AAC Codec Library for Android. Uses a fork without HE-AAC, HE-AACv2, or xHE-AAC support to avoid patent licensing and GPL compatibility issues when built without the he-aac option.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
