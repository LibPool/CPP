# aklomp-base64

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause, aklomp-base64, c++, vcpkg

## 简介

Implementation of a base64 stream encoding/decoding library in C99 with SIMD (AVX2, AVX512, NEON, AArch64/NEON, SSSE3, SSE4.1, SSE4.2, AVX) and OpenMP acceleration

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
