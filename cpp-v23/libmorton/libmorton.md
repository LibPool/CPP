# libmorton

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, libmorton, vcpkg

## 简介

Header-only library for encoding/decoding Morton codes in/from 2D/3D coordinates

Conan Center 收录：C++ header-only library with methods to efficiently

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
