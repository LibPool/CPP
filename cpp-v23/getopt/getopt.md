# getopt

> 标签: c++, getopt, vcpkg

## 简介

The getopt and getopt_long functions automate some of the chore involved in parsing typical unix command line options.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
