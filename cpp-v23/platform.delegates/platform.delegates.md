# platform.delegates

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, platform.delegates

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：platform.delegates is one of the libraries of the LinksPlatform modular framework, which uses
    innovations from the C++17 standard, for easier use delegates/events in csharp style.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
