# neargye-semver

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, neargye-semver, vcpkg

## 简介

C++17 header-only dependency-free versioning library complying with Semantic Versioning 2.0.0

Conan Center 收录：Semantic Versioning for modern C++

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
