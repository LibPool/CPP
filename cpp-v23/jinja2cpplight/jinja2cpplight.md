# jinja2cpplight

> 标签: c++, jinja2cpplight, vcpkg

## 简介

(very) lightweight version of Jinja2 for C++, Lightweight templating engine for C++, based on Jinja2.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/hughperkins/Jinja2CppLight
- vcpkg 端口：https://vcpkg.io/en/packages/jinja2cpplight
- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/jinja2cpplight
