# absent

> 标签: absent, c++, vcpkg

## 简介

A small C++17 library meant to simplify the composition of nullable types in a generic, type-safe, and declarative way

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
