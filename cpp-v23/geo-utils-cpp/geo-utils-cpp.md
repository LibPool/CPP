# geo-utils-cpp

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, geo-utils-cpp, vcpkg

## 简介

Header-only C++17 library for spherical (lat/lng) geometry: distance, bearing, area, point-in-polygon

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
