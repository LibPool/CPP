# simde

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, simde, vcpkg

## 简介

Implementations of SIMD instruction sets for systems which don't natively support them

Conan Center 收录：Implementations of SIMD instruction sets for systems which don't natively support them.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
