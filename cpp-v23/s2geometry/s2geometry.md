# s2geometry

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, conan, s2geometry, vcpkg

## 简介

S2 is a library for spherical geometry that aims to have the same robustness, flexibility, and performance as the very best planar geometry libraries.

Conan Center 收录：Computational geometry and spatial indexing on the sphere

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
