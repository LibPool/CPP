# cgns

> 标签: Zlib, c++, cgns, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

The CFD General Notation System (CGNS) provides a standard for recording and recovering computer data associated with the numerical solution of fluid dynamics equations.

Conan Center 收录：Standard for data associated with the numerical solution

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
