# parallel-hashmap

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, conan, parallel-hashmap, vcpkg

## 简介

A header-only, very fast and memory-friendly family of C++ hash map & btree containers.

Conan Center 收录：A family of header-only, very fast and memory-friendly hashmap and btree containers.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
