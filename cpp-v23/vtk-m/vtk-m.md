# vtk-m

> 标签: c++, vcpkg, vtk-m

## 简介

VTK-m is a toolkit of scientific visualization algorithms for emerging processor architectures.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
