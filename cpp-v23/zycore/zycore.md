# zycore

> 标签: MIT, c++, vcpkg, zycore

## 简介

Provides platform independent types, macros and a fallback for environments without LibC.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
