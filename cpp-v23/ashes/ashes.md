# ashes

> 标签: MIT, ashes, c++, vcpkg

## 简介

Drop-in replacement to Vulkan's shared library, allowing the use of OpenGL or Direct3D11 in addition to Vulkan.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/DragonJoker/Ashes

- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/ashes
