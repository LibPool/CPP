# nanobench

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, nanobench, vcpkg

## 简介

Simple, fast, accurate single-header microbenchmarking functionality for C++11/14/17/20

Conan Center 收录：ankerl::nanobench is a platform independent
                     microbenchmarking library for C++11/14/17/20.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
