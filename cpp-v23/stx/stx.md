# stx

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, stx, vcpkg

## 简介

STX is a collection of libraries and utilities designed to make working with C++ easier and less error-prone.

Conan Center 收录：C++17 & C++ 20 error-handling and utility extensions.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
