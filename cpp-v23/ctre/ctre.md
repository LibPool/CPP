# ctre

> 标签: Apache-2.0, Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception, c++, conan, ctre, vcpkg

## 简介

A Compile time PCRE (almost) compatible regular expression matcher

Conan Center 收录：Compile Time Regular Expression for C++17/20

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
