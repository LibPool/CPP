# tinyspline

> 标签: MIT, c++, tinyspline, vcpkg

## 简介

Library for NURBS, B-Splines, and Bézier curves, allowing you to handle splines with ease

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
