# qcbor

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, qcbor, vcpkg

## 简介

Comprehensive, powerful, commercial-quality CBOR encoder/ decoder that is still suited for small devices.

Conan Center 收录：Comprehensive, powerful, commercial-quality CBOR encoder/decoder that is still suited for small devices.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
