# arrow

> 标签: Apache-2.0, arrow, c++, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

Cross-language development platform for in-memory analytics

Conan Center 收录：Apache Arrow is a cross-language development platform for in-memory data

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
