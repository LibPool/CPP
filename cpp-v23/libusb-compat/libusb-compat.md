# libusb-compat

> 标签: c++, conan, libusb-compat

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：A compatibility layer allowing applications written for libusb-0.1 to work with libusb-1.0

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- 官网（Conan）：https://github.com/libusb/libusb-compat-0.1
- Conan Center：https://conan.io/center/recipes/libusb-compat
- Conan recipe 源码：https://github.com/conan-io/conan-center-index/tree/master/recipes/libusb-compat
