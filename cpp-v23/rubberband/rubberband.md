# rubberband

> 标签: GPL-2.0-or-later, c++, rubberband, vcpkg

## 简介

A high quality software library for audio time-stretching and pitch-shifting.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
