# libtasn1

> 标签: LGPL-2.1-or-later, c++, conan, libtasn1, vcpkg

## 简介

A secure communications library implementing the SSL, TLS and DTLS protocols

Conan Center 收录：Libtasn1 is the ASN.1 library used by GnuTLS, p11-kit and some other packages.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
