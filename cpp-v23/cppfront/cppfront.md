# cppfront

> 标签: CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0, c++, conan, cppfront

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：Cppfront is a experimental compiler from a potential C++ 'syntax 2' (Cpp2) to today's 'syntax 1' (Cpp1)

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- 官网（Conan）：https://github.com/hsutter/cppfront
- Conan Center：https://conan.io/center/recipes/cppfront
- Conan recipe 源码：https://github.com/conan-io/conan-center-index/tree/master/recipes/cppfront
