# roaring

> 标签: (Apache-2.0 OR MIT) AND BSD-3-Clause, c++, conan, roaring, vcpkg

## 简介

A better compressed bitset in C (and C++)

Conan Center 收录：Portable Roaring bitmaps in C and C++

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
