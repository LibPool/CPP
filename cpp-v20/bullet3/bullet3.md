# bullet3

> 标签: ZLIB, Zlib, bullet3, c++, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

Bullet Physics is a professional collision detection, rigid body, and soft body dynamics library

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
