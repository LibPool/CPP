# qtgraphs

> 标签: c++, qtgraphs, vcpkg

## 简介

The Qt Graphs module enables you to visualize data in 3D as bar, scatter, and surface graphs.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
