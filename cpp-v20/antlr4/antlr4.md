# antlr4

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, antlr4, c++, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

ANother Tool for Language Recognition

Conan Center 收录：powerful parser generator for reading, processing, executing, or translating structured text or binary files.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
