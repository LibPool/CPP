# sqlpp11-connector-sqlite3

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause, c++, conan, sqlpp11-connector-sqlite3

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：A C++ wrapper for sqlite3 meant to be used in combination with sqlpp11.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
