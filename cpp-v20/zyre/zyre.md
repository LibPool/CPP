# zyre

> 标签: MPL-2.0, c++, conan, vcpkg, zyre

## 简介

An open-source framework for proximity-based peer-to-peer applications

Conan Center 收录：Local Area Clustering for Peer-to-Peer Applications.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/zyre

- Conan recipe 源码：https://github.com/conan-io/conan-center-index/tree/master/recipes/zyre
