# dav1d

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause AND ISC, c++, dav1d, vcpkg

## 简介

dav1d is a new open-source AV1 decoder developed by the VideoLAN and FFmpeg communities and sponsored by the Alliance for Open Media.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- 官网：https://code.videolan.org/videolan/dav1d
- vcpkg 端口：https://vcpkg.io/en/packages/dav1d
- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/dav1d
