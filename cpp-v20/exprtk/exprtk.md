# exprtk

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, exprtk, vcpkg

## 简介

Simple to use, easy to integrate and extremely efficient run-time C++ mathematical expression parser and evaluation engine.

Conan Center 收录：C++ Mathematical Expression Parsing And Evaluation Library ExprTk

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
