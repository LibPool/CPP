# geos

> 标签: LGPL-2.1, LGPL-2.1-only AND (Apache-2.0 OR BSL-1.0), c++, conan, geos, vcpkg

## 简介

Geometry Engine Open Source

Conan Center 收录：GEOS is a C++ library for performing operations on two-dimensional vector geometries.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
