# lz4

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause, c++, conan, lz4, vcpkg

## 简介

Lossless compression algorithm, providing compression speed at 400 MB/s per core.

Conan Center 收录：Extremely Fast Compression algorithm

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
