# rebound

> 标签: GPL-3.0, c++, conan, rebound

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：REBOUND is an N-body integrator, i.e. a software package that can integrate the motion of particles under the influence of gravity.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
