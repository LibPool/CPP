# gz-sensors

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, gz-sensors, vcpkg

## 简介

Component of Gazebo, provides numerous sensor models designed to generate realistic data from simulation environments.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
