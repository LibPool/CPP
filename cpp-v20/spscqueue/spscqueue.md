# spscqueue

> 标签: c++, spscqueue, vcpkg

## 简介

A bounded single-producer single-consumer wait-free and lock-free queue written in C++11

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
