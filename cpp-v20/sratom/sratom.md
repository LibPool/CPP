# sratom

> 标签: ISC, c++, sratom, vcpkg

## 简介

Sratom is a library for serialising LV2 atoms to and from RDF, particularly the Turtle syntax.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
