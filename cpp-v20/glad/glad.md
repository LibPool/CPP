# glad

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, glad, vcpkg

## 简介

Multi-Language Vulkan/GL/GLES/EGL/GLX/WGL Loader-Generator based on the official specs.

Conan Center 收录：Multi-Language GL/GLES/EGL/GLX/WGL Loader-Generator based on the official specs.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
