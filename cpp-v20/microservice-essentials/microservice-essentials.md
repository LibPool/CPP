# microservice-essentials

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, microservice-essentials

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：microservice-essentials is a portable, independent C++ library that takes care of typical recurring concerns that occur in microservice development.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
