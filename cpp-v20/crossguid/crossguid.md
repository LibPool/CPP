# crossguid

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, crossguid, vcpkg

## 简介

CrossGuid is a minimal, cross platform, C++ GUID library.

Conan Center 收录：Lightweight cross platform C++ GUID/UUID library

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
