# openni2

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, openni2, vcpkg

## 简介

OpenNI is open source library for access to Natural Interaction (NI) devices such as RGB-D camera.

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- 官网：https://github.com/OpenNI/OpenNI2
- vcpkg 端口：https://vcpkg.io/en/packages/openni2
- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/openni2
