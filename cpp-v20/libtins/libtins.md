# libtins

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause, c++, libtins, vcpkg

## 简介

High-level, multiplatform C++ network packet sniffing and crafting library

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
