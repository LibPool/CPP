# libprotobuf-mutator

> 标签: Apache-2.0, c++, conan, libprotobuf-mutator, vcpkg

## 简介

Library for structured fuzzing with protobuffers.

Conan Center 收录：A library to randomly mutate protobuffers.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
