# libev

> 标签: BSD-2-Clause OR GPL-2.0-or-later, c++, conan, libev, vcpkg

## 简介

libev is a high-performance event loop/event model with lots of features.

Conan Center 收录：A full-featured and high-performance event loop that is loosely modelled after libevent

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
