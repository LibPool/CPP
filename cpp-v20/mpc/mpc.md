# mpc

> 标签: LGPL-3.0-or-later, c++, conan, mpc, vcpkg

## 简介

GNU MPC is a C library for the arithmetic of complex numbers with arbitrarily high precision and correct rounding of the result.

Conan Center 收录：GNU MPC is a C library for the arithmetic of complex numbers with arbitrarily high precision

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
