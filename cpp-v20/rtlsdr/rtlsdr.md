# rtlsdr

> 标签: GPL-2.0-or-later, c++, rtlsdr, vcpkg

## 简介

rtl-sdr is a library that turns your Realtek RTL2832 based DVB dongle into a SDR receiver

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。

## 官网

- 官网：https://osmocom.org/projects/rtl-sdr
- vcpkg 端口：https://vcpkg.io/en/packages/rtlsdr
- vcpkg 源码：https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg/tree/master/ports/rtlsdr
