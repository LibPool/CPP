# playrho

> 标签: Zlib, c++, conan, playrho

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：Real-time oriented physics engine and library that's currently best suited for 2D games.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
