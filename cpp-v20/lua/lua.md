# lua

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, lua, vcpkg

## 简介

A powerful, fast, lightweight, embeddable scripting language

Conan Center 收录：Lua is a powerful, efficient, lightweight, embeddable scripting language.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
