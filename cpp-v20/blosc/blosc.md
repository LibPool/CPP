# blosc

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause, blosc, c++, vcpkg

## 简介

A blocking, shuffling and loss-less compression library that can be faster than `memcpy()`

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
