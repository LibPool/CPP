# cminpack

> 标签: c++, cminpack, vcpkg

## 简介

A C/C++ rewrite of the MINPACK software (originally in FORTRAN) for solving nonlinear equations and nonlinear least squares problems

本端口来自 vcpkg 官方端口集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
