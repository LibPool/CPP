# geographiclib

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, geographiclib, vcpkg

## 简介

GeographicLib, a C++ library for performing geographic conversions

Conan Center 收录：Convert geographic units and solve geodesic problems

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
