# dpp

> 标签: Apache-2.0, Apache-2.0 AND BSD-2-Clause AND BSD-3-Clause AND MIT, c++, conan, dpp, vcpkg

## 简介

D++ Extremely Lightweight C++ Discord Library.

Conan Center 收录：D++ is a lightweight and efficient library for Discord

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
