# seadex-essentials

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, seadex-essentials

## 简介

Conan Center 收录：essentials is a small c++ library that offers very basic capabilities for applications and libraries.

本库来自 Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
