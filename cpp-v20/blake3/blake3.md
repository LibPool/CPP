# blake3

> 标签: CC0-1.0 OR Apache-2.0, CC0-1.0 OR Apache-2.0 OR Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception, blake3, c++, conan, vcpkg

## 简介

BLAKE3 cryptographic hash function.

Conan Center 收录：BLAKE3 is a cryptographic hash function that is much faster than MD5, SHA-1, SHA-2, SHA-3, and BLAKE2.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
