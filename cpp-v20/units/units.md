# units

> 标签: MIT, c++, conan, units, vcpkg

## 简介

A compile-time, header-only, dimensional-analysis and unit-conversion library for C++23 with no dependencies.

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
