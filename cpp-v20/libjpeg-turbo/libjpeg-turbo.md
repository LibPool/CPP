# libjpeg-turbo

> 标签: BSD-3-Clause AND IJG, c++, conan, libjpeg-turbo, vcpkg

## 简介

libjpeg-turbo is a JPEG image codec that uses SIMD instructions (MMX, SSE2, NEON, AltiVec) to accelerate baseline JPEG compression and decompression on x86, x86-64, ARM, and PowerPC systems.

Conan Center 收录：SIMD-accelerated libjpeg-compatible JPEG codec library

本库来自 vcpkg 官方端口集、Conan Center 官方 recipe 集，已收录于 cpp-v11、cpp-v14、cpp-v17、cpp-v20、cpp-v23。
